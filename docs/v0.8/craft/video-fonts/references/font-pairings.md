# Font registers and pairings for video

Font registers are the voices a video can speak in: institutional, technical, loud, editorial, geometric, warm or
speculative. Pick the register from the content, then a display face and a text face that disagree on purpose. Every
family below is free under the SIL Open Font License on Google Fonts unless noted; check weights on the family page
before use.

## How to choose a register

How to choose a register: name the voice ("a trusted bank", "a terminal at 3 a.m.", "a hand-painted shop sign"),
imagine the type as a physical object the brand could ship, and reject the first face that comes to mind if it is
the one every video uses for that register. An editorial brief does not need a serif; a technical brief does not need
a mono.

## Pairing rules

Pairing rules that hold across registers:

- Cross a category boundary (serif with sans, sans with mono) unless one family is held apart by extreme weight.
- One expressive face per scene; the other recedes.
- Similar-but-different faces (two geometric sans) read as a mistake.
- Give different communicative modes different voices: statements, data and attribution.
- In video, time is hierarchy: what appears first reads as most important, whatever its size.
